What kanji do you know?
You can choose from either a standardised list or your own.

Available predefined lists:

  • Japanese School Grades
  • JLPT levels or the revised version
  • Kanji Learner's Course
  • Jouyou (2020 revision)
  • Frequency in media
  • Kanji Kentei or the 2020 revised version
  • Remembering the Kanji - 1st Edition or 6th Edition
  • Kanji in Context
  • Hadamitzky/Spahn book
  • Wanikani
Your Kanji Source
Choose the list from which you have studied or are studying kanji, and the levels you have completed. If you wish, you can then enter the number of kanji you know. The kanji will be arranged in the grid in the same order as they appear in this list!
Select levels...
Leave empty to include all kanji from your selected levels. Or enter a number to specify exactly how many kanji you know (e.g., entering "500" means you know the first 500 kanji from the list).
Paste the kanji you know
Tip: You can paste entire sentences, paragraphs, or vocabulary lists. Only kanji will be extracted and counted!
Comparison Target
Select a target learning list and the levels you want to compare. The grid will show you which kanji you already know, which ones you still need to learn, and any extra kanji you've learned that aren't in the target list.
Select levels...
The grid will highlight which kanji you know vs. which ones appear in your target levels.
